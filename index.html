About Us

Hylar Herbs & Body Wellness is a Nigerian wellness brand owned by Olumade Ruqoyyah Ajoke, it's dedicated to providing natural and herbal wellness products designed to support healthier lifestyles and everyday wellbeing.

Founded with a passion for natural wellness, Hylar Herbs & Body Wellness serves customers in Nigeria through its locations in Ede, Osun State and Lagos, while making its products accessible to customers beyond these locations.

The brand offers a range of wellness products across areas including feminine wellness, fertility and TTC support, men’s wellness, infection care, body wellness and other herbal wellness solutions.

Hylar Herbs & Body Wellness is committed to combining traditional knowledge of natural herbs with carefully developed wellness products and a customer-focused approach.
