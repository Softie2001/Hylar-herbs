How to Choose Herbal Wellness Products Safely in Nigeria

Herbal products vary widely in their ingredients, preparation, quality, and intended use. Before buying, it helps to ask a few practical questions about what is in the product and how it should be used.

1. Check the full ingredient list

Look for the names of all ingredients and their quantities where available. “Natural” does not automatically mean safe. Some herbs can cause side effects or interact with medicines.

2. Read the label and directions

Check the stated serving size, instructions, warnings, batch information, storage guidance, and expiry date. Ask the seller for clarification when information is missing.

3. Check regulatory information

Where registration or authorization is applicable, verify the product details with Nigeria’s National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC). A seller’s claim of approval should be independently checked.

4. Be cautious about treatment claims

A product description alone is not proof that it treats or cures a disease. Be especially careful with claims about infertility, infections, diabetes, or other medical conditions. Do not replace prescribed treatment with an herbal product without discussing it with a qualified healthcare professional.

5. Ask before combining products

Consult a healthcare professional before using herbal products if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, take prescription medicines, have a chronic condition, or are buying for a child.

6. Know what to ask the seller

  • What ingredients are in this product?
  • What are the usage instructions and warnings?
  • Is there a batch number, expiry date, or applicable registration number?
  • How should the product be stored?
  • What should I do if I experience a reaction?
Health information: This guide is educational and is not a diagnosis, prescription, or guarantee of results. Seek urgent medical care for serious symptoms or suspected adverse reactions.
