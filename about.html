About Hylar Herbs & Body Wellness

Hylar Herbs & Body Wellness is a Nigerian wellness brand owned by Olumade Ruqoyyah Ajoke. The brand is dedicated to providing natural and herbal wellness products designed to support healthier lifestyles and everyday wellbeing.

Founded with a passion for natural wellness, Hylar Herbs & Body Wellness serves customers in Nigeria through its locations in Ede, Osun State and Lagos, while making its products accessible to customers beyond these locations.

The brand combines traditional knowledge of natural herbs with carefully developed wellness products and a customer-focused approach.
